# Results provenance

- `rcdpi_v1_reported_100.csv` and `rcdpi_v1_reported_action_counts.csv`: transcribed from the user's terminal output for the completed V1 experiment (RCDPI and Rule, 100 episodes each). Raw current-run `episodes.csv` and `actions.csv` were not supplied for this archive. Descriptive means, no significance tests.
- `dql_historical_summary_100.csv`: exact copy from the earlier uploaded `strong42_eta1_analysis.zip` (not the new DQL run in the 300-episode comparison). Do not treat it as the third column of the current comparison.
- The user reported that all 300 formal V1 evaluations finished. The final current-run DQL aggregate and the final raw three-policy archive are not included here because they were not supplied.
- **No USPI closed-loop results exist in this repository.** Unit tests are software verification only, not an empirical policy evaluation. No claim of safety, improved return, or superior stability is made for USPI.
